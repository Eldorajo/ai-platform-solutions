AI Path Switcher

When a customer's firewall or VPN blocks the AI provider, the client stops fighting the network. It probes, detects the block, and switches to a store-and-forward path through your own app, which the network already allows. Turn the firewall on and off and watch both paths.

What to try

  • Direct only, then turn the firewall on: every request hangs at the firewall and fails. This is the failure in the scenario.
  • Auto switch, firewall on: the first request times out, the client switches, and that same request is retried through the fallback. After that, nothing fails. Requests are slower, and they all succeed.
  • Turn the firewall off while traffic runs: the client re-probes the provider every 10 seconds and moves back to the direct path on its own.

The switcher, in TypeScript

Only network blocks trigger the switch. A provider error such as a rate limit is rethrown, because sending it the long way round would not fix it.

This page is a simulation: no network calls are made, and timings are illustrative (direct call 550 to 900 ms, 1.5 s timeout at the firewall, fallback polled every 500 ms). The probe interval is shortened to 10 seconds for the demo; in production it would be about a minute.
